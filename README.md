# Offshore Skipper

A 2D offshore sailing management and racing game that runs in your browser.

**▶ Play it here: https://steezyb.github.io/Offshore-Sailor/**

Build a racing team from a single club boat into a world-class programme. Race inshore regattas, coastal sprints and the great ocean classics. Buy and campaign boats, hire crew, chase sponsors, and climb the world rankings.

## Features
- Live races with real wind, current, polars, sail changes, gear failures and a fleet tracker
- Fleets of up to 60 boats, from 20 ft sportboats to 100 ft line-honours maxis
- Season championships, class divisions, a world ranking, and line honours and handicap results
- The Pacific 52 Super Series, the Nations Cup and the Global Ocean Race around the world
- Rival teams that buy, sell and retire boats, plus a yacht market with boat histories
- Delivery skippers, hosting your own regattas, career milestones and a Hall of Fame

## Controls (in a race)
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ← / → or A / D | Steer (hold Shift for fine steering) |
| ↑ / ↓ or W / S | Trim the mainsheet in / out |
| Space | Tack or gybe |
| T / Shift+T | Faster / slower time warp |
| Backspace | Back to 1× speed |
| Y | Fleet tracker |
| + / − | Zoom |
| 1–9 | Change sail |

Tap any boat or nametag in the race view to see its details.

## Saves
Progress saves automatically in your browser. Use **Saves** in the game to manage multiple careers.

## Running it
It's a single self-contained `index.html` with no build step or server. Open it in any modern browser.
